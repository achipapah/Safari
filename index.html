WELCOME TO RHONDA SPIRIT TOURS

Travel beyond the ordinary.

Rhonda Spirit Tours is a Nairobi-based travel and tour company dedicated to creating unforgettable adventures across East Africa. We specialize in wildlife safaris, primate experiences, beach excursions, honeymoon holidays, group adventures and educational tours across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda.

Whether you dream of watching wildlife roam the savannah, meeting mountain gorillas or relaxing beside the Indian Ocean, we help make your journey memorable.
